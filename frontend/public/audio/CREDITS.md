# Interface sound effects

Creator: **Kenney**
Pack: Interface Sounds 1.0 (2020)
Source: https://kenney.nl/assets/interface-sounds
Download: https://kenney.nl/media/pages/assets/interface-sounds/fa43c1dd4d-1677589452/kenney_interface-sounds.zip
License: **CC0 1.0 Universal** — https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/

Original, unmodified Ogg recordings distributed in this app:

- `click_001.ogg`: buttons and navigation
- `select_001.ogg`: keyboard grid selection
- `switch_001.ogg`: cartridge insertion and seating
- `confirmation_001.ogg`: save loading, successful login, achievement unlocks
- `error_001.ogg`: failed login
- `back_001.ogg`: ejecting a cartridge

The original pack license is included in `KENNEY-LICENSE.txt`. No sounds were synthesized or generated for this project. Effects play at a quiet volume after user interaction; the sound toggle persists across sessions.
